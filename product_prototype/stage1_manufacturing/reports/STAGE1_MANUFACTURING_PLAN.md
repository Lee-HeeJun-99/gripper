# Manufacturing plan — conditional prototype, not fabrication release

1. Do NOT machine revA–G or purchase from this BOM as a released drawing set.
   revH is a checked engineering candidate; resolve release issues first.
2. CNC6061-T6 candidate Palm/Root/back flange/motor angle plate/pedestals/idler
   bracket. Integrated foot/plate shapes must be cut from angle stock/billet,
   not assumed bonded together. Threaded holes and tool-side access need
   released machining drawings before cutting. Chamfer sharp sheet/wire edges.
3. Turn POM candidate drum and idler; drill cross holes and angled terminal
   wire seat. Add real positive wire/shaft retention BEFORE manufacturing.
   Current open angled terminal seat only clears installation; it cannot
   certify pullout or even a complete termination.
4. Grind/turn shaft seats only after bearing manufacturer fit and shoulder/
   axial retention are selected. Do not use16.05 bore as a press fit.
5. Cut PET0.30 sheet and spring strip0.20x3mm, inspect burrs, cracks and actual
   thickness. Candidate alloy301full-hard requires supplier cert and bending
   tests; it is NOT selected/qualified by the word spring in CAD metadata.
6. Purchase dimension-verified motor, bearings, fasteners after drawings and
   current availability/lead times are checked. No purchases performed here.

Proposed shop requirements, NOT measured process capability: machined datum
features±0.05mm unless otherwise stated; sheet outline±0.10; remove burrs;
coaxial bearing seats inspected on common shaft gauge. Bearing radial seats,
axial shoulders, race contact land and endplay require supplier-specific
limits; no universal tolerance is substituted. Printed prototypes may be used
for hand assembly only, not bearing-seat/load/fit certification.

Before powered Stage2: instrumented tendon pullout, shaft/drum torque-slip,
nut retention, clamp sheet-slip, bearing running torque and axial pullout.
Record actual material lots, gauge uncertainty and failures. Reference test
loads must be derived from Stage2 required forces and safety policy, not
invented material allowables. Current1.5N/.012375Nm are reference inputs only.
Competition-rule acceptance UNKNOWN until exact competition/version is given.
