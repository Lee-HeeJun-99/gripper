# Current manufacturing route — Rev AA mechanical prototype

Controlled package and release decision must accompany the complete STEP,
face catalogue, interface limits, BOM and current assembly guide. Do not
machine from an old RevH screenshot or all-edge SVG alone. Procurement
requirements are not actual material certificates or stock confirmations.

1. NEW6061-T6 Palm and Root/motor/carrier parts: billet CNC, not bonded
   imaginary angle plates. Machine critical datum faces first; fixture paired
   carriers in their mounted position for line-boring16.030±0.005 and common
   axis0.010DIAMETER. Preserve actual wire/dock slots and critical webs.
2. Main303shaft/custom axles/pin: turn, ream, grind journals and shoulders;
   inspect5f6maximum-material envelope/common coax0.005DIAMETER. Cut specified
   fine threads/runouts, gauge mating nuts, deburr crossholes without reducing
   pin-bearing lands. No arbitrary standard fully-threaded axle replacement.
   Main shaft outboard grip is4.400/-0.010, NOT5f6; actual5f6 bearing seats
   and fully formed M5zones are separately dimensioned. Manufacture actual
   runouts under washers. Pair NEW stepped coupling jaws on common fixture:
   pilot4.45output, counterbore5.05input toY-18.5, transition<=0.20axial.
   Inspect extra nut plainGO4.42/NO_GO4.70 and actual6H; these acceptance
   requirements are not fabricated supplier minor-diameter measurements.
3.6061Drum: turn U groove and flanges, machine accessible2mm-endmill rounded
   terminal pocket from+Y and all declared cap/pin holes. Machine annular cap
   separately; assemble positive pin/plugs before Idler access is obstructed.
   POM-C Idler turned/reamed on common axis. ACTIVE broad U floor/root:
   ground custom form tool1.10overall/0.90flat/0.10corners (not0.90total),
   actual floorD21.850..21.860, flatwidth>=0.900; inspect20±1°C. Finite tool
   and eight radial approach positions checked; actual machining capability,
   feed/runout/chatter/tool wear require shop acceptance, not fabricated data.
4.303 split receiver halves: rough from billet, machine split/datum faces,
   pocket/exit and axle/hole axes; machine actual0.6deep head/nut recesses.
   Final3D ball finish actualR0.35ends using documented NS MRB230R0.35x4 or
   proven equivalent. Under-neck4mm is NOT4mm flute. Actual finite cutter and
   taper/shank reach checks passed geometrically. Fixture support/runout,
   feeds, burr control and0.45rim inspection remain shop setup obligations.
   Hex-pocket cornerR<=0.15 and AF4.10±0.02; do not round antirotation faces
   or leave a nominal sharp-corner assumption in the machining program.
   Mill actual outer heel pockets0.5depth, internalR0.5 with1mm endmill,
   preserving bores/seats. Conditional pivot/bolt web floors2.325/1.225;
   half remaining thickness2.39. Actual finite40cutter poses clear. These
   are selected geometry bounds, not measured clamp/hinge strength.
5.6061-T6 terminal parts: machine actual front1mm inner relief and lower leading
   fork relief, then true90°DIN7991 countersinks after connecting bridges.
   Inspect retained bearing cone/index surfaces and0.50lateral web. Paired
   fork bores reamed on common axis; do not force axles through misalignment.
   Manufacture stationary receiver compression sleeves and inverted Idler
   shoulder axles/metal washers. Match/lap thrust shims to actual hub/body
   widths and stationary spans for0.025..0.035mm total running gap and groove

   Root/tip clamps retain the stack; do not add a full-span structural bond.
