# Integrated status: NOT QUALIFIED

Actual executed studies are linked in STAGE2_TASKS.json. They include304-solid
geometry integration, conditional six-wrench statics, work-conjugate cable path,
linear beam/FEM references, finite rotation/stroke/energy, actual shaft3D FEA,
time-converged two-inertia control, dimensional corners and finite candidate CAD.

They do NOT yet form one validated continuous physical path. The motor running
curve, certified load cap, actual coupling contact/preload, complete inertia,
finite pin/drum bearing pressure, end pullout, cableEA/drag, laminate slip and
local structure remain unqualified. Independent gate never promotes coupon PASS.

New critical contributions: internal layer clamp forces can greatly exceed
Tendon force; rigid-coupling alignment can overload a motor despite generous
assembly clearance; nominal holding torque admits a jam reference much larger
than the no-object closing demand. These must be resolved before a digital release.

No Rev AA Native SOFA or full100g test was run. No old B3 PASS was transferred.
All Stage1 files and old failures remain unchanged; only separate candidate
and test fixtures were created. No product CAD candidate is adopted by this report.
