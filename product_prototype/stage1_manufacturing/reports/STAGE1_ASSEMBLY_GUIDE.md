# Unpowered assembly/service sequence — candidate revH

No motor power until shaft/axial/wire retention and controller guards close.

1. Inspect Palm slots, holes and datum; deburr all wire/sheet-facing edges.
2. Assemble motor to its integrated angle plate using fourM3x6 candidate
   screws. Nominal3mm thread entry in4.5mm vendor hole; verify actual stack,
   pilot length and screw tip before tightening. Install at this step BEFORE
   shaft/bearing/pedestals: complete assembly blocks the long driver approach.
3. Fasten angle plate foot to Palm with specified M3 base bolts/nuts; inspect
   both sides. Washers, torque and locking policy remain release items.
4. Stage shaft/coupler/drum/bearings outside the Palm. This step cannot be
   completed as released hardware yet: bearing axial retention and torque
   locks are missing. Do not substitute glue/friction or a virtual joint.
5. Mount the pedestal pair using M3x50 candidate bolts/nuts only after Step4
   is resolved. Check axis alignment without bearing side-load, free spin
   and actual endplay. No press force on an unsupported bearing race.
6. Assemble fixed idler bracket, spacers, pulley and axle nut; verify free
   rotation and that a threaded shank is not an unqualified bearing surface.
7. Insert PET/rail stack upward through Palm slot. Attach Root front/back
   clamp at side bolts. No unspecified adhesive blocks cartridge service.
8. Attach terminal halves through perforated sheet. Threading of the wire
   follows upper drum/idler common tangent, left departure and angled seat.
   Final tendon clamp/stop is NOT designed to release level; stop here.

Actual service check: disconnect/unload wire first; remove F0_ROOT_FRONT,
F0_ROOT_BOLT*, F0_ROOT_NUT*. Remaining Palm, back clamp and all drives stay.
Move complete sheet/rails/terminal hardware downward120mm.25 saved poses
are collision-free in service_revH_completed. This is NOT continuous CCD,
full hand clearance, physical nut retention or a powered maintenance test.
Reverse assembly uses the same discrete path; wire reconnection remains open.

Actual driver check uses R2.25x29.9mm straight shank ending0.1mm before screw
head: no hex recess/handle/hand is modeled. Full assembly has blocked paths;
only declared pre-pedestal assembly cut-set passes. See raw result per screw.
