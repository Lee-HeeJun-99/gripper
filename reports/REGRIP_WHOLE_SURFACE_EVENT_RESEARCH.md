## Implemented numerical model

`SurfaceEvent.h` searches every actual triangle with ClosestPrimitive's
vertex/edge/face branches. The numerical dense path is
`x(a)=rest+u0+h*a*v0+h*a*a*(v_mid-v0)`, where v_mid solves the
implicit midpoint mechanical stage. Its derivative defines pre-impact velocity.
This is a collocation trajectory, NOT the exact continuous FEM solution and
NOT interpolation of stored frames. A maximum nodal path-speed bound permits
conservative advances of minimum gap/speed. Roots are bracketed locally;
`clear_until` and the event fraction delimit the unresolved tolerance interval.
All entering roots indistinguishable within the declared time/gap resolution
are collected. Degenerate geometry, unresolved grazing and exhausted search
budgets reject instead of inventing a normal or denominator.

Important limitation: root gap arithmetic is double precision,not a complete
interval CCD proof. Follow-on527/528 adds interval nondegeneracy certification
for the polynomial birth path;continuous-ODE and sustained-path validity
remain outside that certificate.
Whole-surface here means all triangles in the chosen numerical path, not a
general certified continuous-ODE CCD implementation.

`ManyRowImpact.h` solves the perfectly inelastic, frictionless law
`Mr(v+ - v-)=Jr^T p`, `p>=0`, `Jr v+>=0`, `p*(Jr v+)=0`, using
unregularized projected coordinate descent on the positive-semidefinite dual.
Mr and Jr retain the original quarter-root mapping. Limits are explicit:
1024 rows,4096 sweeps; failure is an algorithmic limit,not OS exhaustion.
The existing <=16-row active-subset model remains the independent oracle.
Redundant-row impulses need not be unique; resultant momentum and velocity
are tested separately. No friction/restitution claim is made.

`WholeSurfaceMidpoint.h` uses the existing C2 ControlProfile at actual
midpoint/event times; no duplicate control formula exists. Smooth unique
event branches include incoming-velocity and C2 start-time IFT sensitivities.
Independent simultaneous normals do not have a claimed unique event tangent.
`FilmRestartHistory` records the discontinuity. The new smooth integrator is
one-step midpoint, NOT validated BDF2 resume. Old BDF2/BE models are preserved.

`SustainedSurface.h` is a separate EXPERIMENTAL split scheme: existing BE
variational position solve plus candidate closure, followed by mass-metric
unilateral velocity impulse projection. It is NOT unchanged BE kinematics.
Position correction impulses, velocity projection impulses and numerical
projection energy loss are separate inventories. That loss is NOT physical
impact heat,Coulomb heat or viscous dissipation. Initial inward touching
states without a detected birth still explicitly reject. General release/
recontact and swept clearance during sustained stages remain unqualified.

`FilmWholeSurfaceEventOwner` integrates actual Assembly mass,geometry,mapping
and triangles. It rejects moving other bodies/additional masses. Event rows
have event-owned IDs; sustained rows have step/stage-owned IDs, triangle,
feature,barycentric,normal,gap,actual reduced Jacobian and multiplier journals.
Native IDs are not reused. `FilmSurfaceODESolver` is the public SOFA ODE
entry for the complete owner step, before end-step ForceField recorders.
It replaces neither the installed SDK nor the old BDF solver. The alternative
post-end correction path remains separate and is not used for refinement.
