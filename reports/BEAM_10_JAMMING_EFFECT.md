# BEAM10 — what the new evidence says about jamming

Status: **PASS elastic structural coupling effect; pressure-induced grasp benefit
NOT RUN/unsupported.**

New100mm2/4/8layer k0/k100 structural results are in BEAM04. Ratios are computed
only after both states pass equilibrium. Spring k is not relabeled vacuum
pressure. Adding layers at fixed per-layer section adds material and cannot
by itself quantify the effect of jamming or a same-material design advantage.

New three-finger +Tz uses the inherited pressure/friction research parameters.
It establishes one finite-resolution equilibrium and balance subgate, not that
pressure improves holding. No new matched pressureOFF/low/medium/high load-limit
or torque-limit campaign was run because contact/history convergence failed.
The prior observation that small-load ON/OFF differences were below mesh error
remains historical, not contradicted or promoted to a new benefit claim.

Pressure distribution,real film μ,vacuum leak,damage,hysteresis and tendon path
require actual measurements/calibration. No such measurements were supplied.
Structural stiffness gain,contact geometry changes,and grasp wrench capacity
must remain distinct metrics in the next campaign.
