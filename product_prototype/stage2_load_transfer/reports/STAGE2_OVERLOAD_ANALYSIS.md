# Jam-load envelope is NOT protected

`validation/worst_case/jam_reference1`: manufacturer nominal holding0.45Nm /
minimum seated radius11.165mm gives an ideal40.3045N cable reference.
This is NOT measured stall torque or a guaranteed safety upper bound. It shows
why an unprotected low-N closing study cannot set the maximum design load.

Equal-tension triangle bound gives idler80.609N;2.75mm lever to1.55mm runout
produces nominal606.35MPa bending, before notch/preload/fatigue. Factor2 would
require1.213GPa yield.303typical241MPa is NOT an allowable, but certainly not
evidence that this reference case is safe. A stroke stop cannot protect a jam
that occurs before the stop. Uncontrolled coupling slip is not a safety limiter.

An independent certified torque/tension cap or redesigned rated drive is needed.
Manufacturer candidates inspected, NOT purchased/selected:

- [HPI friction units](https://highprecisioninc.com/torque-limiters/):0.01..40
  **in-lb**, not Nm. Low-torque range is relevant; actual model dimensions,
  accuracy, bores and thermal behavior require a manufacturer drawing.
- [Huco401.25](https://www.huco.com/shop/couplings/miniature-friction-clutches/401-25):
 26mm outer diameter, minimum6mm bore,2deg maximum backlash; not a drop-in
 5/4.4mm interface. Maximum torque is not minimum calibrated release torque.
- [Dynatect Slip-Ease12](https://dynatect.com/product/slip-ease-slip-clutches/):
 19.05mm OD and31.75mm adjustable shaft-to-shaft length, metric5mm standard bore;
 published0.96Nm is capacity, NOT a certified low setting. Current19.5mm-long
 coupling envelope is insufficient; accuracy at a low torque needs confirmation.

Next useful task is a force-cap mechanism/interface requirement and candidate
screen, not arbitrarily assuming a motor current corresponds to a safe torque.
New explicit overload and alignment criteria were ADDED to the release gate;
all original thresholds remain unchanged. No Stage3 or powered approval.
